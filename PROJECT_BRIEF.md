# Bluewell Ventures — Ecommerce Project Brief

## 1. Brand Identity & Strategy
- **Brand Name:** Bluewell Ventures
- **Industry:** Artisanal Specialty Tea & Estate Coffee
- **Personality & Vibe:** Bold, Urban, Fresh & Artisanal (modern brew culture, premium daily rituals)
- **Primary Audience:** Discerning urban coffee and tea drinkers across India

## 2. Catalog & Pricing Rules
- **Launch Catalog:** 4 Hero SKUs (Monsoon Malabar Coffee, Darjeeling First Flush Black Tea, Chikmagalur Arabica Coffee, Kashmiri Saffron Kahwa Tea).
- **Price Range:** ₹400 to ₹4,000.
- **Dynamic Sizing:** Grams & Kilograms options (100g, 250g, 500g, 1kg, 2.5kg) with live price updates.

## 3. Store Architecture
- `index.html` — Hero banner, value propositions, featured blends, customer praise, newsletter.
- `shop.html` — Full catalog with category filters (All, Coffee, Tea) and sorting.
- `product.html` — Dynamic product view (`?id=1`), weight selector, tasting notes, brew guide, reviews.
- `cart.html` — Real-time cart calculations, coupon engine, UPI & COD checkout, instant WhatsApp order routing.
- `about.html` — Sourcing ethos, estate direct philosophy, urban brew mission.
- `contact.html` — Inquiry form, direct support email, response commitments.
- `admin.html` — LocalStorage-backed orders dashboard with fulfillment status updates.

## 4. Visual Design System
- **Forest Green:** `#1B3B2B` (Brand anchor, nature, freshness)
- **Terracotta Clay:** `#C85A32` (High-conversion accents & buttons)
- **Rich Mocha/Espresso:** `#221812` (Headings & deep typography)
- **Warm Sand/Cream:** `#FAF7F2` & `#F0EBE1` (Soft, organic surfaces)
- **Typography:** 'Outfit' (Headings) & 'Plus Jakarta Sans' (Body)
- **Touch Target Standard:** Minimum 44px tap targets for mobile usability.

## 5. Operations & Fulfillment
- **Shipping Policy:** FREE shipping on orders above ₹999. Flat ₹70 for orders below ₹999.
- **Payment Modules:** Integrated UPI scan placeholder (`bluewellventures@upi`) + Cash on Delivery (COD).
- **WhatsApp Integration:** Instant order dispatch via WhatsApp API link directly connecting buyer and seller.
- **Support Channels:** Floating WhatsApp button on all pages + `hello@bluewellventures.com`.
